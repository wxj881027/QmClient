## What this controls

In-game crosshair size, labeled "Ingame cursor scale". The crosshair changes shape with your weapon; the slider sets only its drawn size, not where bullets fly.

## In game

The crosshair anchors your eye's landing point. Too small and you cannot tell where you point; too large and it covers the block underfoot, hurting hooks and close-range calls.

Weapons change the sprite, so the same setting looks different per weapon — size it by the one you hold most.

On death, spectating or demo playback, your own crosshair is gone; what is drawn is the spectated player's crosshair, governed by "Show spectator cursor" — not the slider failing.

## Watch out

0 means not drawn at all, not the smallest size.

Around 30 is still visible; below that, feel takes over.

The grenade helper's landing marker also scales with this value, by a separately limited factor, never as large as the crosshair.
