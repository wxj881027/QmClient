## What this controls

A small decorative Tee trailing and circling behind your own Tee, rendered with a skin you pick, existing only on your screen.

## In game

- It hovers to the side above your head and bobs gently over time; when you move or hook, it follows on a spring. After a teleport or respawn, if it is too far away it disappears first, then fades in from the new position.
- Its emote follows your current emote, and its facing transitions between your mouse direction and movement direction; in free spectate it looks at the camera center instead.
- It appears only in online games and replays; while spectating your Tee is not on the field and the pet fades out — the setting is not broken.
- Nobody else sees it, and in replays it is drawn only on your own screen.

## How to tune it

- After turning on "Show the pet", tune the look with "Pet size" (10–500%, default 60%) and "Pet alpha" (10–100%, default 90%).
- "Pet Skin:" takes a skin name (default twinbop) and does not interact with your main skin.
- Too big or too opaque blocks the terrain underfoot and people passing by; lower the alpha first, then the size.

## Watch out

- The pet, trails and rainbow are all pure visual drawing; when frames drop, turning this kind of feature off first pays off the most.
