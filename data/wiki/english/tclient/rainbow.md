## What this controls

Replaces the colors of the Tee's body, feet and blood particles with a cycling hue; the same colors can also take over the hook and weapons. Purely local rendering — others do not see it.

## In game

- Everything taken over on screen shares one hue; the rainbow cannot be used to tell yourself apart from others.
- With "Rainbow others" off, only your own Tee cycles colors; the same applies to the hook and weapons.
- Black mode is pure black, not dark — in dark maps you can barely see the Tee.

## How to tune it

- "Rainbow Tees", "Rainbow weapons" and "Rainbow hook" control the takeover range independently.
- "Rainbow speed" is a percentage (default 100%); at 0 it freezes on the current color. The slider is logarithmic, so the high end spans a wide range.
- Pick the mode in the dropdown: Rainbow cycles the hue continuously; Pulse jumps the hue in whole seconds with a brightness breathing on top; Black stays constant pure black; Random picks a new pair of random colors each second and blends between them.
- To tell friend from foe: leave "Rainbow others" off and rely on team colors or the warlist.

## Watch out

- Colors are written into the skin's custom coloring, which overrides the skin's palette; the skin's texture shapes stay unchanged.
- Once a weapon is taken over, the weapon skin's colors are no longer visible either.
