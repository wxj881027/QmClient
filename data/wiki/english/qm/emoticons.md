## What this controls

Two toggles for other players' emoticons, plus a key that switches emote launch mode. It only decides how your client displays emoticons sent by others, and whether your emote key places them over your head or fires them outward.

## In game

- With "Show other players' large emoticons" off, the charged, enlarged emoticons others release above their heads no longer appear.
- With "Show other players' launched emoticons" off, the emoticons others fire from their Tee toward the crosshair no longer appear; both launch kinds (normal and large) count as launched.
- To launch emoticons yourself, first switch into launch mode: press "Launch emote key", then pick an emoticon with your usual emote key. A launched emoticon flies for about 5 seconds and disappears.

## How to tune it

1. If large overhead emoticons merely block your view of the map, turn off "Show other players' large emoticons" only.
2. For clean recordings or screenshots, turn both off.
3. Bind "Launch emote key" to a key you do not need elsewhere. Press it to switch modes, then choose an emoticon with your usual emote key; for a large launched emoticon, hold for about 1.5 seconds to charge fully before selecting it.

## Watch out

- Turning display off only affects what you see. Others can still send everything, and you still see your own effects as usual.
- The launch key switches the mode, not the emoticon; while the mode is on, every emoticon you pick is launched.
- The global emote switch (`cl_showemotes`) and the ignore list apply before these two toggles; with the global switch off, nothing here has any effect.
- Players on vanilla DDNet clients cannot see launched or enlarged emoticons — that is not your toggles failing.
- Your client keeps at most 64 launched emoticons at once; when full, a new one replaces the one with the least time left.
