## What this controls

A small glass panel pinned to the top right of the HUD, up to four lines: the current key stuck, hammer, dummy control and dummy copy state. All four toggles default on and there is no master switch; to hide the block you untick items one by one.

## In game

Check your state while practicing key sticking and hammerfly without opening a menu:

- The key stuck line reads On / Off / Reset Self.
- The hammer line shows the hammer mode: Normal / DF / HDF / Custom.
- The dummy control and dummy copy lines read On or Off.
- Line color follows state: green normal, yellow warning, red abnormal. With "HUD rainbow colors" on, all four lines turn rainbow and stop changing with state.

## How to tune it

Practicing key sticking only? Keep the first line. Practicing hammerfly? Keep hammer and dummy control. Once it is all muscle memory, untick items to leave the corner empty and turn them back on for review.

## Watch out

- The panel shows config values, not live presses or the hammer animation: the key stuck line reads `cl_dummy_reset_on_switch`, the hammer line reads `qm_deepfly_mode`.
- In replays you see the values recorded when the demo was captured; changing your config now does not change the replay display.
