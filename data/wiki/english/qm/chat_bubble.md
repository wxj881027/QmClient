## What this controls

A rounded bubble above each player's head showing their most recent chat message. It draws on the nameplate layer; the chat log itself is untouched.

## In game

When a teammate calls "wait for hook" or "back off", you can see who is talking without scanning the chat box. While you type in the chat box, your bubble shows the draft character by character, then swaps to the sent message. Each bubble stays up for the "Duration" (1–30 seconds, default 10 seconds) and leaves with the selected animation; a player only ever has one bubble, and a new message simply replaces the old one.

Text entering a bubble passes through the word filter and the streamer sensitive-word list first, so matched words are masked in bubbles too.

## How to tune it

1. Turn on "Show chat bubbles above players".
2. A "Duration" of 3–5 seconds is usually enough for one glance; leaving them up too long fills the screen with old talk.
3. Tune "Bubble opacity" (default 80%) and "Font size" together; semi-transparent small text gets in the way of terrain the least.
4. "Animation" offers Dissolve, Shrink and Bounce. Pick "Background color" and "Text color" to match the map's brightness — no pure white background on bright maps.

## Watch out

- With Zen mode's "Hide player messages" ticked, bubbles vanish along with the chat, regardless of the bubble toggle.
- Bubbles draw above the head; in narrow corridors they cover the terrain above and hook-point judgement, so switch them off before running hard maps.
- Only the most recent message is shown; miss it and the chat log is all that is left.
