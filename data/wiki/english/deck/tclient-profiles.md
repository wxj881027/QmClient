## What this controls

One identity stored as an entry: skin, body and feet colors, emote, name, clan, flag. One press later loads it back — onto the main tee only, or onto the dummy only.

The card has two tabs: "Profiles" manages the entries, "Bind Wheel" turns a selected profile into a slot on the wheel.

## In game

The moment you load, you change: nameplate and scoreboard switch with you. With name included, the room shows the new name immediately, no rejoin needed. The "After Load" preview on the card is what you will look like once it lands.

## How to tune it

1. First tick which fields to include. By default only "Save/Load Skin", "Save/Load Colors" and "Save/Load Emote" are ticked; name, clan and flag need your own tick.
2. Set your current look, then press "Save" to store it as an entry.
3. Select an entry and press "Load", or double-click it or press Enter to load directly.
4. For the dummy only, tick "Dummy" before saving and loading.

| Goal | How |
|---|---|
| Swap names often | Tick "Save/Load Name" |
| Clear the clan on load | Tick "Overwrite clan even if empty" |
| Edit an old entry | Turn on "Enable Deleting", set the look, press "Override" |
| Put it on the wheel | Press "Add to bind wheel" |

## Watch out

- A profile holds exactly these six items; it is not a backup of your whole setup.
- Unticked fields are neither written nor cleared, so the same profile can load differently on the main tee and the dummy.
- "Delete" needs "Enable Deleting" first, and deleted entries have no recycle bin.
- Loading repeatedly in game keeps sending identity updates to the server; everyone sees you change name and skin nonstop.
