## What this controls

The two voting keys: "Vote yes" and "Vote no", sending a yes or a no to the server.

## In game

The vote bar shows your bound keys directly: "Key - Vote yes" on the left, "Vote no - Key" on the right. A press casts the vote, the server counts it into the running vote, and the bar marks your side.

With no vote running, a press does nothing at all.

## Watch out

You cast a choice, not a reason; vote counts and pass conditions are the server's rules — locally you only change keys. Votes often pop up mid-run, so both keys must stay reachable while holding hook.
