## What this controls

The skin browser: search box, All / Favorites / Recent tabs, sorting, and a thumbnail grid that applies skins directly.

## In game

Clicking a row applies the skin to whichever side (player or dummy) is selected in the preview above — no need to go back and retype the name. Each cell has two icons: a star to favorite, a plus to queue.

Skins change looks only, never hitboxes. Cells stay empty until a skin file has finished decoding; scroll fast and you see a batch of placeholders — stop, wait a frame, then judge.

## How to tune it

1. Narrow the field with the search box first, then star the skins you want living in "Favorites".
2. Sorting works on "All" and "Favorites" only: by "Name" to find a specific one, by "Time" for the newest.
3. A single click applies to the current side; to skip the selection and target a side directly, use the two double-click shortcuts.

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Single-click a row | Applies to the currently selected player or dummy |
| Left double-click | Applies to the player regardless of the current selection |
| Right double-click | Applies to the dummy |
| Star | Adds to / removes from Favorites; records the skin name, not colors |
| Plus / square minus | Adds or removes this skin together with its colors in the queue |

Right of the search box, four tool buttons in order: skin database, skin directory, edit skin texture, refresh; refresh also works with F5 or Ctrl+R.

## Watch out

The search term is stored in the config and greets you again next time. Skins seem to have vanished? The filter is probably still set — press "Reset filter" in the empty list.

"Recent" holds up to 20 entries, only skins you switched to or recolored manually; skins swapped out by queue rotation don't count. The clear button cannot be undone.

Favorites record the name only — one entry no matter how many color variants; "Recent" records colors too, so different palettes of the same name each take a slot.
