## What this controls

The bind card for seventeen miscellaneous actions: scoreboard, statboard, spectate, emote, pie menu, two consoles, screenshot, the practice switch, several display toggles, and "Active disconnect", which drops the connection outright.

## In game

Hold to show: scoreboard, statboard, emote, pie menu, spectate mode. On release you return to the game, and the emote and pie menu also trigger the item your pointer rests on at release.

One press per toggle: console, remote console, entities display, HUD, scoreboard cursor, fast practice. With entities on, normal terrain stops drawing and only doors and switches remain, for lining up positions; the HUD toggle blanks the whole display, press again to restore.

Sent to the server: "Lock team" posts a /lock into chat; the team locks only if the server knows that command.

"Spectate mode" shows its selector only while spectating; holding it mid-game pops nothing. "Spectate teleport" works only in free view, jumping the camera to the other end of the teleporter group under the mouse.

"Fast practice" is a local switch, one press in or out, without the server's /practice vote; inside practice, the kill key returns you to where practice started instead of dying.

"Active disconnect" cuts the current connection at once, logged as an abnormal disconnect; the client itself stays open.

## How to tune it

| Action | Trigger | Where to put it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scoreboard, statboard | Hold | Somewhere handy |
| Emote, pie menu | Hold to aim, release to trigger | Hand zone your pointer reaches well |
| Spectate mode | Hold while spectating | Apart from the toggles |
| Console, screenshot, the two display toggles | One press | Away from movement keys |
| Active disconnect | One press, no confirmation | Deep inside, never adjacent to movement |

## Watch out

Active disconnect has no second confirmation — one press drops main Tee and dummy together.

"Emoticon" will not pop while the scoreboard is open, while spectating, or in a demo. "Pie menu" is off by default; enable "Enable pie menu" first, and it stays hidden while chat or console is open.

"Scoreboard cursor" only works with the scoreboard open; in demo playback it does nothing.

"Lock team" is a chat command; on a non-DDNet server it does not exist and nothing happens.

One key carries one command; overwriting kills the old action at once — check the other cards after rebinding.
