## What this controls

The DDNet upstream credit list: a row of contributor names spaced by interpuncts, with two buttons above, "DDNet staff" and "DDNet releases".

## In game

Nothing in matches. The list is fixed at compile time, reads no files and never goes online, so it shows in full even offline.

## Watch out

The list is a snapshot of the build moment; people added upstream appear only after a client update.

Both buttons open ddnet.org pages, maintained by DDNet; the client neither caches nor embeds them.

Nothing here is changeable — finding no switch is normal.
