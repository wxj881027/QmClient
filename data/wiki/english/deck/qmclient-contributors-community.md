## What this controls

The community entry: a line reading "QQ group: 1076765929 (click to copy)", with two buttons below — "Join QQ group" and "View latest updates".

## In game

You never touch it in a match, and it changes no settings. It takes you from the settings page to the group or the updates page; the rest happens in the browser.

## Watch out

Clicking the line only writes the number to the system clipboard, the text turns into "Copied", and it flips back after one and a half seconds. The client does not record whether you joined; next time it looks the same.

Both buttons open in the system default browser; no reaction usually means a browser or network problem, and the card itself has no state to retry.

The group number is hard-coded in the client; if the group ever changes, you wait for a client update.
