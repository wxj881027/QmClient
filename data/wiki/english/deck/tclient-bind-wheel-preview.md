## What this controls

The wheel as it looks right now: a ring split evenly by entry count, slots growing under the mouse, the selected one larger and light green.

## In game

It mirrors the in-game ring, but the two sides judge hits differently. The preview counts a hit from a quarter of the radius inward; the game has a dead zone within 110 distance of the center. Selectable in the preview does not mean the pointer will stop there in game.

## Watch out

- Left-click selects a slot and fills its name and command into the fields on the left; right-click swaps it with the selected slot; middle-click only selects, without touching the fields.
- Clicking inside the innermost quarter clears the selection and empties both fields.
- You are reordering memory only; saving the config writes it to disk. No undo.
- Long names overlap each other. If a slot is unreadable, check it in the input fields.
