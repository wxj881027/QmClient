## What this controls

Draws left, jump and right arrows beside each name, showing what every Tee is currently pressing. "Show players' key presses" has four levels: None, Own, Others, All.

## In game

Your arrows come from local input and light up as you press. Other players' arrows come from the server-synced direction and jump state, so they run half a beat behind and may stay lit briefly after the player releases.

Only these three icons — fire, hook and weapon switch never show.

## How to tune it

On practice maps pick "Own" to see where you press an extra step or miss one; "Others" for spectating and replay review; "All" to read the whole room. Icons covering the name: adjust "Size of key press icons".

| Level | Who gets drawn |
| --- | --- |
| None | Nobody |
| Own | Only you, dummy included |
| Others | Only other players |
| All | Everyone |

## Watch out

Changing this page during a replay shows no difference; set the key-press level on the Demo card instead.

On crowded servers "All" puts icons on every nameplate — watch your framerate while spectating.
