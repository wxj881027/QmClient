## What this controls

Lines up the hook collision line's colors and widths in five fixed sample rows, so you can check settings without joining a server.

## In game

The rows, in order: nothing hookable, hookable tile, a player, the same line drawn with "Width of others' hook collision line", and one with the tip segment at the end. Each row has your skin on the left, tile, dummy or tip segment on the right.

"Preview 'Hook collisions' being pressed" below only changes the preview, never the config: on, the lines switch to the colors they would have while the hook key is held, to check whether pressed and normal states separate at a glance.

## Watch out

Sample length is fixed, not the map's hook length; judge color and width only, don't measure with it.

The dark backdrop is deeper than a real map; on light maps, trust the in-game contrast.

While tile textures are still loading, only lines are drawn, no tiles — the colors aren't gone.
