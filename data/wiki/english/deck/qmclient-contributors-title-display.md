## What this controls

How the title badge is drawn on your screen: whether it appears on name plates, beside or above the name, its size, color and shape, and whether sponsor chat effects are shown in chat. Local display only; the title text is never changed.

## In game

Turn off "Show titles on nameplates" and the small icon badge above other players' heads is gone; chat titles are unaffected.

"Show titles above player names" moves the badge from beside the name to its own line above. In crowds, name and badge squeeze together; above-name reads better.

"Show main tee title locally" and "Show dummy title locally" filter only your own two connections; everyone else stays as is.

Turn off "Show sponsor chat effects locally" and the soft glow and highlight effects stop being drawn on your own screen; other players' displays are unaffected.

## How to tune it

1. "Title style" has two options, Moon and Sun. If the color is still the previous style's default when you switch, it follows to the new style's default; a color you set yourself is kept.
2. "Title color" tints the whole badge — background, border, text and icon together, not just the text.
3. "Nameplate title size" runs 50–200%, default 100%, scaling with the name font size.
4. "Title opacity" defaults to 100%; lowering it fades the whole badge.
5. Check the "Title preview", then press "Save" when satisfied.

## Watch out

- You edit a local draft here; the preview changes at once, but in-game name plates use the server's saved copy — without "Save", only you see it.
- The title text comes from the server; a nickname mismatch or an expired title is not drawn.
- The "Save" at the bottom of both title cards is the same action, each submitting title, bound nickname, style and badge appearance as one package.
