## What this controls

Three enlarged outline rings around a Tee, colored by team, so sides are readable in a fight. It changes no skins and no hitboxes and only affects your own screen.

## In game

Grouped players glow in team color; the outer ring fades, the inner one is strongest.

Teamless players follow "Team 0 glow": off, their Tee's body color, a color picked in "Team 0 glow color", or rainbow. The rainbow hue cycles every ten seconds with a different phase per player, so a teamless crowd shows a spread of colors, not one.

Super players never glow.

With the warlist enabled and a match found, the list color is drawn instead and the team glow yields.

## How to tune it

The master switch "Team tee glow" is off by default. Once on, "Team tee glow intensity" defaults to 80% and "Team tee glow halo size" to 40%. Intensity sets how solid each ring is, size how far the halo spreads. The "Team 0 glow color" row only appears when "Team 0 glow" is set to "Custom color".

## Watch out

Intensity at 0 shows nothing while the master switch still looks on — it works, it just looks broken.

All three rings are enlarged, translucent redraws; they cost fill rate as the player count grows, and size costs more than intensity.

"Team 0 glow" set to "Off" gives teamless players no glow at all — the setting working, not a missed draw.
