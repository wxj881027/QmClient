## What this controls

Five volume levels: "Sound volume", "Game sound volume", "Chat sound volume", "Map sound volume", "Background music volume". All are 0–100 numeric fields, all defaulting to 30. With the master switch off, this card is not shown.

## In game

"Sound volume" is the outermost stage; changing it drags every category along. The four categories each govern their own lane: game sounds are jump, gunshot and hurt; chat sounds are those few ding tones; map sounds are the map's built-in mechanism sounds; background music is the menu and loop tracks.

## How to tune it

- Set "Sound volume" at your ceiling first, then balance with the four categories.
- Do not push "Game sound volume" down; landing spots and hurt rely on it.
- Keep "Chat sound volume" at just audible, so a teammate's message never covers footsteps.
- "Map sound volume" at 0 means no mechanism sounds; keep a little if you want the hints.
- "Background music volume" only matters in the menus.

Each item is a numeric field with a slider: type a value directly or drag; holding Ctrl and scrolling works too.

## Watch out

- A category at 0 is not its switch off: 0 is only silence, the events still play; a switch off means they are not played at all.
- "Sound volume" multiplies the already mixed result; with several lanes maxed, pushing further hits the distortion ceiling, not more loudness.
- Map sounds also require "Enable game sounds"; volume alone cannot bring them back.
