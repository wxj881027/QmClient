## What this controls

Seven chat keys: Chat, Team chat, Converse, Chat command, Show chat, Repeat message, Voice chat.

## In game

The channel is fixed the moment the key goes down: "Chat" enters global, "Team chat" enters your team — no switching inside the input box.

"Converse" pre-fills /c and a space, continuing the whisper partner from your last message; "Chat command" pre-fills only a slash, you still type the command name yourself.

"Show chat" expands the history while held and returns to the usual fade-out on release; it does not open the input box.

"Repeat message" does nothing on one press: press twice within 1 second to resend your last sent message verbatim, channel included. With "Enable repeat" off (the default), pressing does nothing at all; when there is nothing to repeat, you get a notice.

"Voice chat" is push-to-talk: hold to speak, release to stop. Without voice enabled, pressing produces no sound.

## How to tune it

Give "Team chat" a key of its own, on the other side of the keyboard from "Chat", so a rushed call-out cannot reach the wrong channel. Put "Chat command" where you will not hit it by accident: it pre-fills one slash, and any line sent starting with a slash is executed by the server as a command, not broadcast as chat.

To rebind, click the key field beside the action, then press the new key. While waiting it shows "Press a key…"; Esc cancels, right-click or the trash icon clears the slot.

## Watch out

While the input box is already open, pressing another chat key does not switch channels — send first, or close it with Esc.

In demo playback chat keys cannot open the input box; you can only read the existing history.

One slot carries one command; once a chat action takes the key, whatever used that key before is gone.
