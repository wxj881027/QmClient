## What this controls

Switches and name colors for the top-right kill and finish lines. "Show kill messages" and "Show finish messages" each control one type; the two colors below only color the names.

## In game

Lines stack downward from the top right, at most five at once, each dropping off after ten seconds. Your name uses "Highlight Color", others "Normal Color" — which line concerns you is clear at a glance.

Time differences have fixed colors: faster than the record is green, slower is red, unchangeable.

## How to tune it

Keep kills on and finishes off while practicing; reverse for demo times. Give your name a color that pops, keep others near the HUD's pale tones so it doesn't outshine the timer.

## Watch out

Colors are written into the text at creation; changing them leaves lines already at the top right untouched until they expire or the window resizes.

Switching off only stops drawing; messages still arrive. In demo playback, these lines follow the switches too.
