## What this controls

A set of binds for movement and common actions: Move left, Move right, Jump, Fire, Hook, plus the hook collision line, zoom, show toggles, Pause and Kill.

## In game

Jump and Hook are held states: they last while held and end the moment you release, with the hook pulling back on the spot.

Fire differs. Shotgun, Grenade and Laser repeat while held; Hammer and Pistol fire once per press and never combo.

"Hook collisions" draws the directions the hook could grab while held and disappears on release; hit detection is untouched.

"Show others", "Show all" and "Pause" each send one server chat command — they only take effect once the server accepts it.

"Zoom in", "Zoom out" and "Default zoom" move the local camera. When the server forbids zooming, these three do nothing except while spectating.

"Toggle dyncam" makes the camera follow the crosshair, and "Toggle ghost" hides or shows your run ghost — both one-press local switches.

"Kill" is blocked by "Client-side self-kill protection": with protection on (the default) you die normally; with it off, a press only shows a notification and sends nothing.

## How to tune it

Bind "Jump", "Hook" and "Fire" first, where your hand never has to move; align left/right movement with your finger habits, and don't share hand space with your chat key. Zoom and show toggles are low-frequency — one or two combos are enough.

To rebind, click the key bar next to an action and press the new key. While waiting it shows a press-any-key prompt; Esc cancels, and right-click or the trash icon clears the slot.

## Watch out

One slot holds one command only. Once a new action takes the key, the old one dies immediately — check your other cards after rebinding.
