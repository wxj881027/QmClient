## What this controls

Visibility of the main in-game HUD and scoreboard colors: the HUD master switch, the health, armor and ammo bars, score, local time, the spectator cursor, the votes window, and name colors in the scoreboard and friends list. Positions are not set here — HUD layout editing handles them.

## In game

Turn off "Show ingame HUD" and the health, armor and ammo at the top left plus the score and timer at the top right all disappear, but chat, name plates and the scoreboard stay — those are separate components.

"Show spectator cursor" only works while spectating others or watching a demo: it draws the spectated player's crosshair position and current weapon shape, pinned to the screen edge when they aim off-screen.

With "Show votes window after voting" on, the window doesn't close itself after you vote, so you can see the results come in.

"Show local time always" shows the time without opening the scoreboard; otherwise it only appears while the scoreboard is open.

## How to tune it

To check what is left on screen, switch the master off and turn items back on one by one — the fastest way to sort out which elements belong to the HUD.

The four color rows under the scoreboard control, in order: names with admin rights, people in the same clan, friends in the friends list, and clan members in the friends list. The first two rows only affect the scoreboard.

## Watch out

"Show health, shields and ammo" also needs the server's consent: when the server declares these two blocks hidden, the switch does nothing.

While spectating, the values you see belong to the spectated player; your own only show once you are back to your own Tee.

Demo playback and video export follow the HUD switches in replay display, not "Show ingame HUD" here.
