## What this controls

The color and weight of the small icons in the interface: icons on the settings page, menus, the Dynamic Island and the in-game HUD all count. Icons only — text is not touched.

## In game

- "UI icon color" picks white or black, and all normal icons take that one color; pick Rainbow and the color keeps flowing around the hue wheel.
- With "Use custom UI icon color" on, the custom color overrides the white, black or rainbow chosen above.
- The friend heart and the favorite star always keep their own colors and never follow the rainbow; "Solid friend icon" and "Solid favorite icon" only change the fill shape of these two icons.
- "Connection icon color" defaults to "Quality tier", so the Dynamic Island's connection bars change with signal strength; set it to a custom color and it stays fixed, hiding how good or bad the connection is.

## How to tune it

1. Dark panels pair with white icons, light panels with black; if you would rather not think about it, keep white.
2. To make icons stand out, first switch "UI icon style" to Bold or Fill, then consider a custom color.
3. Keep the default teal and mint green for the friend and favorite colors — they are the easiest to recognize while scanning a list.

## Watch out

- When an icon sits too close to its background, the client adds a very faint outline automatically, but a custom color picked too dark is still hard to see.
- After "Connection icon color" is set to a custom color, a worsening network no longer gets a color hint — only the bar count.
- "UI icon style" has four steps: Light, Regular, Bold and Fill, with Bold as default; Light is clearly thinner at small sizes.
- Changing "UI icon style" only changes the icon glyphs; the weight of body text is not affected.
