## What this controls

The results area below the search page's input box: matched cards are laid out as they are, editable in place, with "Locate" to jump back to the page they live on.

## In game

Results are ordered by the card registry order, not by relevance, so the closest match is not necessarily first. Cards alternate between two columns; this affects only this page and never touches the order you arranged on the original pages.

Cards with a builder render in place — sliders and checkboxes work as usual. Those without show only a description and a "Locate" button.

When nothing matches, the results area shows a single line: "No matching features found. Try other keywords".

## How to tune it

1. With too many results, use "Locate" at the left of each card title — faster than paging by hand.
2. To change values directly, edit them on the search page; it is the same configuration, no need to re-enter the original page to confirm.

## Watch out

Results are not saved, and they cannot be drag-reordered.

Clear the input box and the results area disappears at once, leaving only the input card on the page.

The search page's own two cards never appear in results; the skin card shows either Tee or Tee 7, one at a time, depending on the current server's protocol.
