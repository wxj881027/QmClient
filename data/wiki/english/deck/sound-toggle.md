## What this controls

The sound master switch plus nine category switches. The master switch is "Use sounds"; when it is off not a single sound plays. Category switches only decide which categories can be heard, never loudness.

## In game

With "Enable game sounds" off, jump, land, hurt and hit sounds all vanish, and the map's own mechanism sounds go too; only the chat notification sounds remain.

"Enable gun sound" and "Enable shout when holding fire while in water" each govern one thing: the pistol fire sound, and the shout while holding fire underwater. They take effect separately only when the server has racing sound effects on; on other servers "Enable game sounds" governs both.

"Mute when not active" pushes the output to zero while the window loses focus. The game keeps running, all sound stops, and it returns at once when you switch back.

The four chat switches each govern one type: server messages, regular chat, team chat, mentions. If global chat is too loud, turn off "Enable regular chat sound" and keep team chat and mention sounds.

## How to tune it

Keep "Enable game sounds" on first — jump and hurt judgment depends on it. Then switch off by noise source instead of killing whole blocks.

Only if you want music in the menus turn on "Play background music"; it is off by default.

## Watch out

If sound was off at this launch, turning it back on often needs a client restart before there is real output; the settings page shows a restart hint at the bottom.

With the master switch off, the volume and audio pack cards vanish from the page, and the checkboxes come back as they were when you reopen it.
