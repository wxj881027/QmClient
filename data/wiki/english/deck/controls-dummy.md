## What this controls

Keys for the dummy (your second Tee): toggle control, copy moves, three action keys, and hammer fly.

## In game

"Toggle dummy" hands direction, jump, hook and aim to the dummy; press again to take them back. The state is not written to the config — after restarting the client you always start on the main Tee.

The dummy runs on its own connection; the two Tees can differ in latency and state.

"Dummy copy" makes the dummy copy your direction, jump, hook, aim and pending weapon switch; press to turn on, press again to turn off.

"Dummy control" also flips on one press: when open, the dummy's jump, hook and fire stop following you and are decided by "Dummy jump", "Dummy fire" and "Dummy hook"; without dummy control, those three keys do nothing at all. All three work while held and restore their previous value on release.

"Dummy hammer fly" makes the dummy switch to hammer and swing at you automatically, for hammer fly. Turn it off and the previous weapon comes back.

## How to tune it

On solo duo maps enable "Dummy copy" first and walk the route once to confirm it keeps up; when the dummy should move on its own, turn on "Dummy control" and steer with the three action keys.

Hammer fly only needs "Dummy hammer fly" on; after that, just manage your own Tee. Keep the three action keys and "Toggle dummy" together, away from your movement keys.

## Watch out

When the server disallows dummies, toggling over does not produce a second Tee — do not suspect your keybinds first.

Hold-type keys like "Dummy jump" restore the previous value on release: if it was already on, letting go will not turn it off.
