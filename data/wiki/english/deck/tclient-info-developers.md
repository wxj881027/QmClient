## What this controls

TClient's signature and entry points. The top half holds four buttons: Discord, Website, Github and Support ♥. The bottom half lists five developers, each with a Tee preview, a name, and an arrow to their home page. It changes no settings.

## In game

Nothing changes. This is a pure information card: the buttons only open web pages in your browser and never touch rendering, input or sound in game.

## Watch out

- The list is hard-coded in the client. It does not go online and is not updated live.
- Each Tee wears the skin its developer specified; if that skin is not installed locally, a fallback skin is drawn instead. The buttons keep working.
- "Support ♥" leads to the donation page; payment happens in the browser.
