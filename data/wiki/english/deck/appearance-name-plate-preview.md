## What this controls

A draggable sample nameplate rendered with your current settings. No settings of its own — it only reflects the choices made on the cards above.

## In game

The sample uses your own name and clan; an empty clan shows the "Clan Name" placeholder. Coordinates are fixed sample values like 12.34 and 56.78, not real positions. All three direction arrows light up at once so you can see the icons — that never happens in a real game.

What you can check here: stacking order, whether rows overlap, whether font sizes and spacing fit, what the effects look like, and which line is hidden by your "Show name plates" level.

## How to tune it

1. Decide which lines to show in the "Name Plate" card and turn off the ones you skip.
2. Confirm order and spacing in the preview, watching for overlaps.
3. Enable "Free move" and drag each line into place. Drags are written to the config and used in game too.
4. If it is a mess, press "Reset layout".

"Preview player nameplate" and "Preview dummy nameplate" switch which identity you look at; the two names differ in length, so check both.

## Watch out

Toggling "Free move" on or off zeroes every offset you placed — each time you start from scratch.

"Reset layout" clears offsets only; it does not turn off free move.

Offsets are fixed pixels. After a resolution change, a font size change, or toggling a line on or off, a tidy layout can crowd up again.
