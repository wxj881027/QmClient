## What this controls

Five live samples, one each for rifle, shotgun, laser door, freeze laser and dragger, drawn with the current colors — changes show immediately.

## In game

Colors, width, glow and pulse all follow the current settings, no server needed. The pulse keeps moving there, so you can see it breathe without firing.

The two weapon rows draw the gun bodies in front; the three entity rows draw the door, snowflake or pulley at the end point.

## Watch out

The three entity rows always draw at full strength and barely change when "Glow intensity" is lowered. To see its effect, look at the two weapon rows above.

Sample length is fixed, and the width doesn't narrow over time like in a match — judge color and layering only.

The preview backdrop is translucent black; real terrain is brighter, so trust the in-game contrast.

Gun-type lasers (Gun, Plasma) aren't in the preview; they reuse the rifle or freeze laser colors — confirm them in a map.
